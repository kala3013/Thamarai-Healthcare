Welcome to Thamarai Fertility - Chennai

Thamarai Fertility Center in Chennai offers world-class fertility treatments and women's healthcare services. Our branch is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and staffed by experienced specialists dedicated to providing personalized care.

IUI is often the first line of treatment for many couples experiencing infertility, as it is less invasive and more affordable than IVF while still offering good success rates.

Who is IUI for? IUI is commonly recommended for unexplained infertility, mild male factor infertility, cervical factor issues, and for women using donor sperm.
