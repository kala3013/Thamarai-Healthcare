In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

Advanced reproductive technology helping couples achieve their dream of parenthood with over 5,524+ successful cycles.

What is IVF?

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) is an advanced assisted reproductive technology (ART) where an egg is fertilized by sperm outside the body in a laboratory. The resulting embryo is then transferred to the uterus to achieve pregnancy. IVF is one of the most effective fertility treatments available today.

Thamarai Fertility has been a pioneer in IVF treatment for over three decades, achieving more than 5,524+ IVF successes and giving hope to thousands of couples.

Pioneering Achievement: Thamarai achieved the first ICSI pregnancy with roundheaded sperms in the region - a landmark in reproductive medicine.

The IVF Process - Step by Step

The IVF process typically takes about 4-6 weeks from start to finish and includes several stages:

1Ovarian Stimulation

The woman receives fertility medications to stimulate the ovaries to produce multiple eggs instead of the single egg that typically develops each month. This is monitored through ultrasound scans and blood tests.

2Egg Retrieval

A minor surgical procedure performed under sedation. A thin needle is guided through the vaginal wall to collect the mature eggs from the ovarian follicles. The procedure takes about 20-30 minutes.

3Fertilization & Embryo Culture

The collected eggs are combined with sperm in the laboratory for fertilization. The resulting embryos are monitored and cultured for 3-6 days as they develop.

4Embryo Transfer

The best quality embryo is selected and gently transferred into the uterus using a thin, flexible catheter. This is a simple procedure that does not require anesthesia.

5Pregnancy Test

Approximately 12-14 days after the embryo transfer, a blood test is done to determine if pregnancy has been achieved.

Who is IVF For?

  • Blocked or damaged fallopian tubes
  • Severe male factor infertility
  • Advanced maternal age (over 35)
  • Endometriosis
  • Ovulation disorders
  • Unexplained infertility
  • Previous failed IUI attempts
  • Genetic disorders requiring PGD
