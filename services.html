Our Fertility & Women's Health Services

Complete fertility solutions, diagnostic tests, procedures, and specialty clinics under one roof with ISO & NABH certified facilities.

Fertility Clinic

This clinic offers the entire fertility services under one roof with experts in the field offering compassionate care. From basic fertility evaluation to advanced ART procedures, we provide comprehensive solutions tailored to each couple's needs.

  • Complete fertility evaluation & diagnosis
  • Ovulation induction & monitoring
  • Intrauterine insemination (IUI)
  • In-vitro fertilization (IVF)
  • Intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI)
  • Egg & embryo donation

Andrology Clinic

Male infertility is one of the major causes of a barren marriage. All counseling, tests ranging from sperm DNA fragmentation analysis to advanced treatments are available. Our andrology specialists provide comprehensive male fertility assessment and treatment.

  • Semen analysis & sperm DNA fragmentation
  • Y chromosome microdeletion testing
  • Karyotyping & genetic counseling
  • Sperm freezing & preservation
  • Treatments for male factor infertility
Andrology Clinic
Book Consultation

Genetic Clinic

The Clinic offers a comprehensive approach with genetic counseling and genetic lab under one roof with experts in the field. We provide advanced genetic testing and counseling for couples planning pregnancy or facing recurrent pregnancy loss.

  • Genetic counseling & risk assessment
  • Preimplantation genetic diagnosis (PGD)
  • Carrier screening for genetic disorders
  • Chromosomal analysis & karyotyping
Genetic Clinic
Book Consultation

Endoscopy Clinic

Endoscopy problems affecting menstrual and reproductive health are analyzed by special investigations and treated minimally invasively. Our advanced endoscopy suite allows for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures with faster recovery.

  • Diagnostic hysteroscopy & laparoscopy
  • Operative hysteroscopy (fibroid/polyp removal)
  • Laparoscopic ovarian drilling
  • Treatment of endometriosis
Endoscopy Clinic
Book Consultation

Fetal Medicine & High Risk Pregnancy

Thamarai fetal medicine unit. The care for the unborn fetus has assumed newer dimensions with technological advances. We offer comprehensive fetal assessment and management of high-risk pregnancies.

  • Detailed anomaly scans & fetal echocardiography
  • Nuchal translucency & first-trimester screening
  • Invasive prenatal testing (amniocentesis, CVS)
  • Management of medical disorders in pregnancy

Special Clinics for Women

Reproductive medicine deals with the prevention, diagnosis, and management of reproductive problems. Our special clinics address specific health needs across a woman's lifespan.

  • Adolescent gynecology clinic
  • Obesity & metabolic health clinic
  • Menopause management clinic
  • Cosmetology & aesthetic gynecology
  • Cancer screening & prevention

Neonatology

The care of the newborn in a tertiary level intensive care setup is essential for the continuum of care of the high-risk deliveries. Our expert neonatology team has been able to send home healthy babies of extreme growth retardation (530 gram) and 24-week premature infants. Invasive and noninvasive neonatal ventilation is one of our special services.
